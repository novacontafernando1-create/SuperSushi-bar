## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-gastronomy, futuristic dining identity that merges Japanese omakase precision with cosmic kinetic energy. The aesthetic channels a sophisticated interpretation of ascending power, celestial aura, and modern izakaya culture—deliberately elevated to avoid novelty, nostalgia, or cartoonish tropes.

The visual direction centers on **Cosmic Cyberpunk-Luxe**:
- **Atmospheric Depth:** Deep-space dark fields set an intimate, luxury nightlife ambiance, punctured by controlled kinetic energy blooms (ki gradients and hyper-focused light strokes).
- **Culinary Precision:** Monolithic card structures, sharp typographic hierarchies, and obsidian surfaces emulate premium slate serving plates and custom forged santoku knives.
- **Controlled Radiance:** Glows, borders, and ambient light sources are restrained—acting as structural focal anchors, directional accents, and status signatures rather than decorative clutter.

## Colors

The color system delivers high dynamic range on OLED screens while strictly regulating luminance to preserve an upscale culinary atmosphere.

- **Primary (`#FF7A1A` - Energy Orange):** Represents concentrated Dragon Ki, sear-torch heat, and prime uni/salmon roe. Reserved strictly for conversion paths, active states, key interactive indicators, and checkout triggers.
- **Secondary (`#1677FF` - Electric Blue):** Embodies charged atmospheric flow and technical sophistication. Deployed for operational states, reservation confirmations, beverage selections, and technical interface metadata.
- **Tertiary (`#762DFF` - Cosmic Purple):** Signifies rare omakase reserves, chef seasonal transformations, and transcendent VIP tiers. Used selectively in conjunction with radial gradients and elevated status surfaces.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#090B12` - Deep Space Black):** The primary void. All viewports terminate here to frame ingredients like luminous celestial artifacts.
- **Surfaces (`#191D28` - Graphite Gray):** Elevated container planes. Rendered with subtle alpha-based borders (`rgba(255, 122, 26, 0.15)` or `rgba(22, 119, 255, 0.2)`) rather than solid outlines.
- **Typography Tint (`#F4F5F8` - Ice White):** Primary text color, engineered to prevent bloom against pure dark surfaces while delivering uncompromising legibility.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes clear structural tension between wide geometric headlines, technical data anchors, and frictionless humanized body text:

- **Syne (Headlines & Title Elements):** Exudes modern avant-garde sculpture. The heavy weight creates distinct character without resorting to literal sci-fi clichés.
- **Space Grotesk (Navigation, Pricing, Meta-Tags & Subtitles):** Houses technical culinary specs (e.g., aging durations, thermal notes, cuts, price markers). It brings precision, structural cadence, and tactical confidence.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Body Content):** Provides unencumbered clarity for dish descriptions, ingredient sourcing narratives, and booking flows.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout utilizes an asymmetric 12-column grid on desktop and tablet, collapsing into a single-column stacked view on mobile.

- **Asymmetry & Focal Pacing:** Showcase items and hero dishes break bilateral symmetry by spanning irregular column intervals (e.g., 7 columns for imagery paired with 5 columns for typography and sensory profiles).
- **Responsive Layout:**
  - **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid, `gutter-lg` (2.5rem), `margin-lg` (4rem). Maximum content envelope capped at 1360px.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid, `gutter` (1.5rem), `margin-md` (2.5rem).
  - **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4-column layout, `gutter-sm` (1rem), `margin` (1.5rem). Quick-action bars and omakase course configurators lock flush to the bottom viewport.

## Elevation & Depth

Spatial layering relies on translucent obsidian slabs, linear refraction highlights, and tinted energy hazes rather than traditional neutral drop shadows.

- **Level 1 (Base Plates & Canvas):** Pitch background `#090B12` accented by stationary, deep ambient radial blooms (`rgba(118, 45, 255, 0.08)` and `rgba(22, 119, 255, 0.06)`).
- **Level 2 (Cards & Modules):** Solid `#191D28` or translucent `#191D28E6` with a `16px` backdrop-filter blur. Edges are framed with a hairline `1px` border containing a directional gradient (`rgba(244, 245, 248, 0.1)` on the light incidence side fading down to `rgba(255, 122, 26, 0.05)`).
- **Level 3 (Interactive Flyouts & Overlays):** `#1E2332` with an ambient glow of `0 12px 36px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)` combined with a soft, directional secondary glow `0 0 20px rgba(22, 119, 255, 0.15)`.
- **Level 4 (Ki Flare / Active State):** Concentrated focal illumination applied to key call-to-actions, containing a double ring: an inner crisp stroke of `rgba(255, 122, 26, 0.4)` and a diffuse outer projection `0 0 24px rgba(255, 122, 26, 0.35)`.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates geometric curves tempered by structural rectangular architecture.

- Base interactive containers, menu modules, and image plates utilize `0.5rem` (`roundedness: 2`) to echo precision-cut sashimi blocks.
- Modal dialogues, floating sheet draws, and elevated experience carousels scale to `rounded-lg` (`1rem`).
- Metadata markers, dietary pills, and interactive status orbs employ fully circular `9999px` radii to contrast with linear grid edges, representing concentrated spheres of ki.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Energy Trigger:** Base background `#FF7A1A` with text `#090B12` in `Space Grotesk` Bold uppercase. Active state projects an exterior aura glow (`0 0 24px rgba(255, 122, 26, 0.45)`). Inner padding uses `0.75rem 1.75rem`.
- **Ghost Tactical (Secondary):** Transparent core framed by a `1px` stroke of `rgba(22, 119, 255, 0.4)`. Text in `#F4F5F8`. On hover, the background morphs to `rgba(22, 119, 255, 0.12)` accompanied by an electric aura glow.
- **Floating WhatsApp Concierge:** Fixed position bottom-right anchor. `#090B12` circular chassis bounded by an animated dual-gradient border (`#1677FF` to `#FF7A1A`). Inner icon rendered in high-contrast Ice White, backed by a persistent pulse halo (`rgba(22, 119, 255, 0.3)`).

### Chips & Energy Orb Badges
- **Status Chips:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), framed in `rgba(244, 245, 248, 0.12)`, dark graphite filling, housing a `label-caps` typographical format.
- **Orb Badges (Spice/Transformation Level):** A circular `8px` luminescent bead (`#FF7A1A` or `#762DFF`) positioned directly preceding the label, emitting an ambient glow `0 0 8px currentColor`.

### Cards & Menu Showcases
- Surface `#191D28` with subtle backdrop refraction. Food imagery features a micro-gradient mask fading from transparent to black at the base to ensure label readability.
- **Hover Micro-Interaction:** Upon interaction, card borders shift from muted graphite to an electric blue or ki-orange gradient trace, with the culinary artifact scaling 2% inward.

### Lists & Specifications
- Asymmetrical list layouts with thin divider rules (`1px` solid `rgba(244, 245, 248, 0.06)`). 
- Left column anchors the course sequence number in `Space Grotesk` muted secondary; right column aligns dish name, culinary process markers, and prices in `mono-price` formatting.

### Input Fields & Controls
- Form controls rest in `#12151E` with an inset boundary of `1px` solid `rgba(244, 245, 248, 0.1)`. 
- Focused inputs replace default browser chrome with an active energy outline in `#1677FF` and an ambient diffuse glow of `0 0 12px rgba(22, 119, 255, 0.25)`.
- Radios and Checkboxes: Geometric squares and circles utilizing pure `#FF7A1A` fills upon selection, accompanied by centered micro-pips.
